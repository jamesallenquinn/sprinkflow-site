1.1.8

StableAugust 9, 2026

SprinkFlow v1.1.8 — the Plan Symbol Library, three new themes, and a much clearer seismic result.

New: Plan Symbol Library (Experimental) A searchable library of clean 2D plan symbols. Find a symbol, click it, and a transparent PNG lands on your clipboard ready to paste into Bluebeam, Acrobat or Foxit. Alias search means "siamese" finds the fire department connection and "os&y" finds the gate valve. Four style families (Standard, Bold, Solid and a white-knockout Halo that stays legible over dark hatch), six ink colors, and favorites plus recents. Eight symbols to start, growing each release.

Three new appearance themes Blueprint (prussian cyanotype with pale cyan ink), Vellum (warm drafting paper instead of screen-white) and Graphite (quiet neutral charcoal with brass). Dark and Plain Light are unchanged. Every pairing meets WCAG AA contrast.

Seismic bracing: what's actually limiting you The calc now names the governing element — the weakest link in the load path — with its maximum allowable load, the demand, and why. A GOVERNS badge marks the row so you can see at a glance that upsizing the brace member buys nothing while a 183 lb anchor is the real ceiling. New optional safety margin % display.

Fixed

  • Installing the ODA File Converter is now picked up without restarting SprinkFlow, plus an "I've installed it — check again" button on every prompt.
  • The Review Plans Scan window no longer closes when you press inside it and release outside — that could silently discard a scan.
  • Backflow preventers no longer appear under Check Valve in catalog matches.

Also

  • Explode CAD Blocks now outputs DWG instead of DXF.
  • DWG to PDF lets you choose which xrefs to bind.
  • Four selectable north arrow styles in the vicinity map, in the app and the free web tool.
  • Tools still maturing are now marked "Experimental" rather than "Early Access".

1.1.7

StableAugust 4, 2026

What's new in 1.1.7

8 new cover templates + a preview gallery. The Material Data Submittal and Hydraulic Calc Package cover picker grows from 5 to 13 designs — from copier-proof black & white to design-forward layouts. The new gallery shows live thumbnails, and you can star your favorites to pin them to the top (shared across both tools).

Slip Sheet — visual page editor. New mode in the PDF workspace: your base PDF renders as page preview cards. Click a page to replace it with the next slip-in sheet, drag a sheet onto a page to replace it, or drop it between two pages to insert. Click any placed page to undo.

PDF to CAD — line weights & colors tuned for AutoSPRINK. Converted drawings import with Element Widths pre-filled (~0.5 / 0.25 / 0.1) and colors mapped to AutoSPRINK's palette — no more everything-at-zero. Weight-split layers (-W050/-W025/-W010), stipple & hatch dots on their own -HATCH layer, a 3/32" = 1'-0" preset, and a custom scale box that takes 3/32, 1:50, or a raw factor.

Hydraulic Analysis Report — residential design bases. New System Design Basis selector: NFPA 13 residential sprinklers (19.3.1), NFPA 13R (7.1.1), and NFPA 13D (10.1/10.2), each writing the real code criteria into the report. Residential also joins the occupancy hazard dropdown with basis-aware density (0.1 under 13, 0.05 under 13R/13D).

Preliminary Calculation — Supply Safety Margin. Converts demand pressure into the supply needed for a chosen cushion, measured correctly as a percentage of the supply (supply = demand ÷ (1 − margin)). Auto-fills from your estimate; also available as a standalone tool in the mobile app.

Plus: the web edition (web.sprinkflow.studio) gains the vicinity map, flow test sheet PDF export, and the Slip Sheet tool.
