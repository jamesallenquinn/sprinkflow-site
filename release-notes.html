1.1.18

StableAugust 20, 2026

The UX release: a faster app that remembers where you were.

NEW - Home SprinkFlow opens to your last job with a Resume button, your last three generated packages (open the PDF or its folder in one click), and a clean start for new work. Turn it off in Settings if you prefer the old behavior.

NEW - Command palette Press Ctrl+K anywhere. Type a few letters to jump to any tool, load a saved project, apply a loadout, toggle a datasheet, switch themes, or generate - all without touching the mouse. Press ? to see every shortcut.

NEW - Pin your tools Star the tools you live in and they form a Favorites group at the top of the sidebar, in whatever order you drag them into.

NEW - Undo instead of "are you sure?" Deleting a contractor, loadout, saved project, or datasheet now just happens, with a ten-second Undo and a 30-day trash behind it.

NEW - Settings that earn the name Sections for shop defaults (default contractor, cover template), scanning (per-category toggles), and appearance - including a Compact density mode.

IMPROVED - Plan scan Pipe types with multiple datasheets now offer a choice (Sch. 10: Bull Moose or Wheatland; CPVC: IPEX or Spears) instead of silently picking one. When CPVC is detected, the review offers CPVC hangers: pick a manufacturer, then add all of theirs or choose individually. A note appears when a pipe datasheet also covers its fittings.

BIGGER CATALOG - 111 new verified datasheets Grooved fittings and couplings across nine manufacturers (Grinnell G-FIRE, Reliable, ARGCO, Gruvlok, Shurjoint, Viking, SCI, Anvil, GemLock), SAMMYS rod hangers, and CPVC hangers from nVent CADDY, Anvil and PHD - every link verified to serve the real manufacturer PDF.

1.1.16

StableAugust 17, 2026

Your contractor profiles are safe now, your loadouts fire themselves, and the catalog grew by 97 verified datasheets.

FIXED - Contractor profiles no longer lose their details

Two long-standing bugs could wipe a saved contractor down to just its logo - typing into a new profile was silently discarded, and an unloaded form could overwrite a good profile with blanks. Both fixed. Details now save as you type (no Save click needed), and a profile that ever loses its fields rebuilds itself from the jobs it was used on. Saved loadouts had a similar bug - they were never written to disk at all - also fixed.

NEW - Loadouts fire on plan-scan detections

Give a loadout triggers - CPVC, Riser Assembly, Lateral Brace, and every other concept the scan can name - and when a scan detects one, that loadout's datasheets arrive in the review pre-checked and labelled with where they came from. Build your CPVC hanger set once; every CPVC job pulls it automatically.

NEW - 97 more datasheets, every link verified

Grooved fittings and couplings from Grinnell G-FIRE, Reliable, ARGCO, Gruvlok, Shurjoint, Viking, SCI COOPLOK, Anvil and GemLock - the catalog had only Victaulic before. Plus the full SAMMYS and Sidewinder rod hanger line. Every URL was checked to return the genuine manufacturer PDF.

IMPROVED - Datasheet catalog filters

Filters are on screen from the start - no more clicking Filters to open them - laid out in an even grid, with a new filter to show only datasheets already in your database. Fittings can finally be filtered by manufacturer.

IMPROVED - The scan knows more products

Hanger detection grew from 5 types to 19: CPVC hangers, riser clamps, side beam brackets, C-clamps, retaining straps, wrap-arounds, bar joists, trapeze, eye rods, concrete anchors and more - each defaulting to a sensible catalog pick. "Threaded rod" now brings up all-thread rod, never Sammys - and "Sammy" brings up Sammys, which have datasheets at last. Bare "grooved coupling" spec language now resolves instead of being skipped. Backflow defaults to the Ames 3000SS and lateral braces to the Tolco Fig. 1001.

1.1.13

StableAugust 13, 2026

The plan scan now reads the title block like a person does.

IMPROVED - Plan scan: project name and address

Measured on 35 real plan sets, the detected project name went from right 32% of the time to 93.5% with a subscription, 74% without. Addresses improved too.

What changed under the hood:

  • The title block is located by real glyph positions now, so CAD-produced plan sets - where the old reader saw nothing at all - parse properly.
  • When the sheet is a scan, the title-block area is re-read at high resolution instead of squinting at the whole sheet at once.
  • OCR now ships WITH SprinkFlow. No PDF24, no Ghostscript, no setup - scanned plans read out of the box on any machine.
  • Subscribers: when the local read still is not confident, SprinkFlow sends just the small title-block snippet (never your plan set) to the cloud AI, which reads it like a plan reviewer would. That is the 93.5% path.

The scan is also honest now. Every detected name says where it came from ("Detected from title block, sheet 1" vs "Guessed from PDF filename"). When confidence is low the field stays EMPTY instead of confidently wrong, and the ranked candidates appear as one-click "Did you mean" suggestions.

Engineer letterheads no longer win the project-name slot, notes headings ("SEISMIC DESIGN INFORMATION") are stopped, and a name that repeats in the title block across sheets beats anything that appears once.

CHANGED - Plan Symbols library rebuilt

The library was redrawn against the office fire sprinkler symbols legend so the symbols you copy out match the symbols on your plans. Twenty-two are new and six were redrawn: fire hydrant, post indicator valve, check valve, backflow assembly, pendent head and inspector's test connection.

New in the library: two-way and four-way seismic bracing, pipe hanger, sprinkler with head guard, hydraulic reference point, grooved coupling, thrust block, threaded plug, threaded or grooved cap, change in pipe elevation, local alarm bell, auxiliary drain, upright head, sidewall head, existing head, radial-spoke head, plain gate valve and a two-way fire department connection.

Everything is drawn in the same house style at the same weights, so a legend you assemble out of these reads as one drawing rather than a scrapbook. A new Hangers & bracing category joins the browse rail.

The legend draws flex and rigid grooved couplings identically - the flex one is called out with an FC tag rather than a different glyph - so the library carries one grooved coupling, not two.

The revision delta ships bare now; the number is lettered in by hand, so the symbol is no longer locked to a 1.

REMOVED

The four-way fire department connection, the waterflow switch, the underground pipe linetype and the three overhead run linetypes (main, branch line, arm-over) have been taken out of the library.

1.1.11

StableAugust 12, 2026

Hazen-Williams calculator, and PDF-to-CAD sheet thumbnails that stay readable on big plan sets.

NEW - Hazen-Williams Calculator (Design Tools)

Pick a pipe, type a flow, read the friction loss in psi/ft as you type. An optional length adds the total. Schedule 40, Schedule 10, CPVC SDR 13.5 and cement-lined ductile iron are built in with their actual internal diameters, or enter your own ID. C factors default per NFPA 13 Table 23.4.4.7.1 and stay editable for aged or specialty pipe, with the common values listed on screen.

The shown-work block spells the formula out with your numbers substituted, including the intermediate powers, so a plan reviewer can check it by hand - and it copies to the clipboard in one click. Velocity comes along for free.

Also on the phone: app.sprinkflow.studio has the same calculator under Field Tools.

FIXED - PDF to CAD: sheet cards collapsed on large plan sets

On a 220-sheet set every thumbnail crushed down to a 2-pixel sliver, so the Sheets panel was a stack of blank bars. The grid was splitting the panel's height equally across every row instead of sizing rows to their content, which also meant a large import fired 178 thumbnail renders at once. Cards now hold their proper size at any sheet count and the panel scrolls; a big set queues about ten previews and loads more as you scroll.

FIXED - Feet / Inch on the phone showed a stray field

The web tools had a CSS rule that outranked the browser's own hide behaviour, so the "Multiply by" box stayed on screen in add and subtract mode. Fixed for every tool at once.

NEW - No Contractor option on Material Data Submittals

The Saved Contractor dropdown now has "No Contractor (leave blank)" for jobs where the contractor block should stay empty on the cover, matching the hydraulic tool's existing toggle.

1.1.10

StableAugust 10, 2026

SprinkFlow v1.1.10 — the spacing optimizer now tells you what its answer is based on.

Fixed: max spacing was calculated from hardware you hadn't chosen yet

The Step 3 spacing optimizer answers "the largest brace spacing that passes every check" — but it ran the hardware checks against whatever happened to be left in the form, which could be a concrete anchor when your brace is landing on wood. On one real stretch that was the difference between 40 ft and 9 ft. Not a rounding error, a different design.

Now the answer always states its basis, and updates live

  • Pipe only — the code spacing limit plus the braced-pipe zone-of-influence table (18.5.5.2) for the main you picked in Step 3. Independent of hardware, so it's a true ceiling.
  • Provisional — some load-path checks are in play; it names which are still unpicked.
  • Final — brace member, assembly and fastener all selected.

The readout moved out of the collapsed Step 3 panel and into the context bar, so it stays visible while you pick hardware. It shows the governing element and its allowable load next to the spacing — swap a 3/8" anchor for a 3/4" and watch the max jump from 25 ft to 40 ft, with the weakest link named either way.

Turn it on with the Auto-optimize checkbox in the Spacing Optimizer panel.

Also

  • At Review, if the spacing you applied exceeds what your final hardware allows, the result says so with the component responsible.
  • A longitudinal brace reports "no pipe-based limit — pick hardware" rather than the meaningless 80 ft code ceiling, since Table 18.5.5.2 is a lateral table.

SprinkFlow v1.1.8 — the Plan Symbol Library, three new themes, and a much clearer seismic result.

  • The Review Plans Scan window no longer closes when you press inside it and release outside — that could silently discard a scan.

What's new in 1.1.7

Preliminary Calculation — Supply Safety Margin. Converts demand pressure into the supply needed for a chosen cushion, measured correctly as a percentage of the supply (supply = demand ÷ (1 − margin)). Auto-fills from your estimate; also available as a standalone tool in the mobile app.
