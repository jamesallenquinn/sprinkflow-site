LESSON B8

Elevation profiles & structure

What you'll learn: giving the flat plan its third dimension — modeling the structure and elevating the piping to follow it.

Video coming soon — the written steps below cover everything.

The structure workflow

  1. Work the sequence: columns → beams → roof. Each step builds on the last, and the plan stays the familiar top-down view while the model gains height.
  2. With structure in place, elevate the system: mains and branch lines take their heights from what they hang under.
  3. Drop sprigs and drops where heads need to leave the branch line plane.

Elevation profiles

  • Cut an elevation profile through the system to see the vertical story: pipe under beam, head below deck, the dimensions a fitter needs.
  • Profiles stay live — move the pipe and the profile follows.
Why bother with structure: hydraulics (lesson B7) and fabrication both live or die on real elevations. Model the steel once and every downstream number gets honest.
