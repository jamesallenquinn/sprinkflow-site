LESSON B6

From heads to pipe

What you'll learn: the full studio's guided piping workflow — from a finished head layout to routed mains and branch lines you can edit.

Video coming soon — the written steps below cover everything.

The guided flow

  1. Start Auto Route Piping from a finished head layout.
  2. The guided workflow walks the decisions in order: where the riser feeds, how mains run, how branch lines pick up the heads — you steer at each stage instead of accepting a black-box result.
  3. Generate the routing and review it stage by stage.

Editing the result

  • Grab and move branch lines; connected piping follows.
  • Continue a route from any pipe end, and reroute sections the field will hate before the field sees them.
  • Pipes grow from their ends as you draw — the drafting feel is the point, not a menu maze.
Layout quality in = routing quality out: even branch spacing and aligned heads (lessons B2–B4) give the router straight, obvious runs — the same thing a human designer wants.
