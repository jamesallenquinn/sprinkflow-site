LESSON A4

AI cut-sheet reading

What you'll learn: what SprinkFlow reads on its own, what the AI adds for subscribers, and the right way to correct a miss.

Video coming soon — the written steps below cover everything.

Two layers of reading

  • Local scanning (free, always on): when you drop plans or PDFs in, SprinkFlow scans them on your machine — pattern matching and OCR, nothing uploaded. This is what detects your legend, schedule, and project details.
  • AI enhancement (subscription): for cut sheets and specs, the AI reads the document like a person would — identifying the manufacturer, product, and where it belongs — so new datasheets file themselves correctly instead of arriving as "untitled PDF."

Using it

  1. Add a cut sheet to the catalog (or drop a spec into a project).
  2. Let the reader run — it proposes the item's name and category.
  3. Review what it proposed. You always get the final say before anything lands in a package.

When it misses

  1. Just edit the item — retitle it, move it to the right category. Your correction sticks; the package prints what the catalog says.
  2. Multi-product PDFs (a whole fitting family in one document) are the usual culprit. Give the item the family name and let the TOC entry describe the group.
Privacy, plainly: plan scanning is local. Only the cut-sheet/spec AI enhancement uses a hosted model, it's opt-in by being a subscriber feature, and your documents are never used to train anything.
