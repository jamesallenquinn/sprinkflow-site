LESSON B1

Design Studio: heads on a plan in 10 minutes

What you'll make: a sprinkler head layout over the job's actual background, with live coverage shading proving every square foot is protected.

Video coming soon — the written steps below cover everything.

What the Design Studio is

SprinkFlow Design Studio is our Windows CAD program for sprinkler design — backgrounds, head layout, pipe routing, hydraulics, structure and elevations, in one drawing. It's in early alpha: unfinished on purpose, rebuilt most weeks, and steered by what testers report. Every active SprinkFlow Tools subscription includes access — there's nothing extra to buy. Download it and read what alpha means →

This lesson is the ten-minute version of the job everybody starts with: get a plan on screen and get heads on it.

Step 1 — Bring in the background

  1. Start a new project and import the job's DWG background (or convert a PDF first with lesson A8's tool).
  2. Confirm the drawing scale so distances mean what they say.

Step 2 — Place heads

  1. Pick your sprinkler and its coverage from the setup panel.
  2. Click to place heads. Smart snaps grab ceiling tiles, spacing rhythms, and alignments as you go — fighting the cursor is not part of this workflow.
  3. Watch the coverage shading fill in live behind the heads. Gaps show instantly, before a reviewer finds them.

Step 3 — Check and send

  1. Sweep the plan for unshaded corners and spacing violations.
  2. Save the project file and send it back to the designer or the office.
Something feel wrong on the way through? Press F12 right then. The Studio grabs the screen, lets you circle the problem, and sends it to us with your note. Reports filed from inside the alpha are what set the next build.
